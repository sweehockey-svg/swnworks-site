Sources are the bands or labels on Bandcamp, official Apple Music artwork for the two digital releases, and the identified 2021 LP review. Album titles, artists, dates, catalogue context, JPEG signatures and dimensions were checked. Original file hashes are recorded in cover-manifest.json. Existing cover mappings are retained.
